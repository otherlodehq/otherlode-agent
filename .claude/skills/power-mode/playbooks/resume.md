### Resume

**The trail is the truth. Do not redo finished work.**

1. Pull every repo. Find the trail: the resume note in STATUS, any `wip/`
   branch, a nightwork report or run log, and the git log since.
2. Rebuild the state: what landed, what sits on the `wip/` branch (diff it
   against the default branch), and what was decided. Treat a recorded
   decision as settled. Do not grill it again.
3. Name the resume point. Do not rerun a review or a repro the trail
   records as done. Verify only what the trail does not show as verified.
4. Route the rest to its playbook. When the `wip/` work is finished, merge
   it to the default branch through that playbook. Luke deletes the branch.
5. Remove the resume note from STATUS once the work lands. Wrap-up.

redid and why (ideally nothing), and the next step.
